## Market Gap

### What Exists
- 🎨 **Diagramming tools** (Lucidchart, draw.io)
  - ❌ Can't run the design
  - ❌ Can't cost it
  - ❌ Can't break it on purpose
  
- 💰 **Pricing calculators** (AWS Pricing Calculator)
  - ✓ Can cost it (from a form)
  - ❌ Can't reason about trade-offs
  - ❌ No simulation
  
- 🔧 **DevOps/IaC** (Terraform, AWS CDK)
  - ✓ Deploy the infrastructure
  - ❌ No feedback on capacity/cost BEFORE building
  - ❌ No interactive design
  
- 📊 **Monitoring** (Datadog, Prometheus setup)
  - ✓ Observe what you built
  - ❌ No visibility during design phase

### What's Missing
A tool that **designs + simulates + costs + generates code**.

ArchSim is uniquely positioned to be that tool.
